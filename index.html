KIMBRANDESIGN is a strategic brand transformation studio led by Kim Sentis.

With over 15 years of experience in cosmetics, branding and marketing, Kim partners with brand leaders to clarify vision, unlock new positioning and guide meaningful transformation.

Combining strategic clarity, emotional intelligence and creative leadership, KBD helps brands express who they truly are – clearly, consistently and beautifully.

We often work with brands at pivotal moments – when they need to redefine who they are and where they are going.

Our hybrid model allows us to assemble tailor-made teams around each project, delivering the strategic depth of a consultancy with the creative impact of a leading agency.

FAQ

Our approach

Do you work on brand strategy and large-scale projects?

+

Yes. We partner with brands on strategic transformation — from positioning and architecture to creative expression across all touchpoints. Our model is built for scale.

Are you specialised in packaging?

+

Packaging is one of our core strengths. We design structural and graphic packaging that elevates shelf presence while staying true to brand essence.

Do you work beyond packaging?

+

Absolutely. Our expertise spans brand strategy, visual identity, retail experience, digital content, and creative direction. We take a holistic view of brand expression.

Do you collaborate with R&D or innovation teams?

+

We frequently collaborate with R&D and innovation teams to ensure brand strategy is embedded from the earliest stages of product development.

Are you a large agency?

+

We are a highly expert and agile structure, surrounding ourselves with bespoke talents depending on each project's scale and complexity.

What differentiates you from a traditional agency?

+

Our founder-led model ensures senior involvement at every stage. We combine the strategic depth of a consultancy with the creative excellence of a top agency — without the overhead.

What is your working process?

+

We begin with deep listening and strategic immersion, then move through iterative creative development with continuous client collaboration, ending with polished delivery and brand guidelines.

Do you work with responsible brands?

+

We are drawn to brands with purpose. Responsibility and positive impact are values we share and actively seek in our partnerships.

Do you work internationally?

+

Yes. We work with global brands across markets, adapting strategy and design to cultural contexts while maintaining brand coherence.

How do you embody your values beyond client projects?

+

Our commitment extends beyond brand strategy. Through initiatives like The Providence Club, we create spaces for dialogue between leaders, creatives and innovators around the positive transformation of brands.

We also contribute to events and reflections on the future of branding – fostering a more human, conscious and inspired ecosystem.
